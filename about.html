Who We Are — Chi Siamo

Small by design. Honest by conviction.

Peaking Waters LLC is a self-funded, sustainably run venture built around one piece of land in Blowing Rock, NC — and one instinct we didn't have to learn, because it was already ours.

Bari on one side. The Veneto on the other. Two families, one instinct — that land is worth farming, water is worth stewarding, and a good glass is worth pouring for someone who shows up. We brought that instinct to a ridge in the Blue Ridge, not the Alps, but the spirit travels.

The Ethos — Il Nostro Modo

What we mean when we say we're going about this differently.

Peaking Waters exists at the intersection of land, water, and technology. We're not chasing scale for its own sake — we're building ventures that are rooted in place, funded by their own conviction, and meant to still make sense in twenty years. That shapes every decision, not just the mission statement.

How We Work — Come Lavoriamo

The journey is the reward — but here's the actual process.

The Land

A single property near Blowing Rock, NC, farmed and stewarded directly — not leased out, not subdivided, not rushed toward a faster return.

The Water

One well, tested on a regular schedule per North Carolina guidelines, with a full source dossier published to every partner before a bench sample is ever made. See the geology behind it.

The Partnerships

We supply the source, the brand, and the demand. Certified co-packing partners supply the bottling capacity. The entire process is published, A to Z — see Co-Pack Partners.

The Pace

Water, Fraser firs, a pet-friendly outdoor space, and seasonal events are sequenced carefully, one at a time, so nothing competes with getting the fundamentals right first.

In Short — In Breve

Rooted in place, built for the long game — because that's how a mountain, and the people who take one seriously, have always done it.
