Chi siamo

A place that's better every time you come back.

Peaking Waters is a mountain property near Blowing Rock, North Carolina, at 4,000 feet and a few minutes from Appalachian Ski Mtn. It's open for camping, picnics, and quiet weekends, and it grows a little every season — new fire pits, new trails, new places to sit.

Come up for a night, a picnic, or a long weekend with the people you love — and leave with a reason to come back.

Per cosa

What this place is for.

Everything on the property is built around three simple things people come to the mountains for.

Slowing down

Porch views, shaded picnic tables, and a quiet garden for reading, prayer, and reflection. Nothing to rush toward.

Gathering

Fire pits, open space, and soon a covered pavilion — room for reunions, retreats, and the kind of dinners that run late.

Being outside

Trails through the woods, tent sites on the ridge, winter overnights, and a pond on the way. Adventure at whatever pace suits you.

Come funziona

How the property is cared for.

Kept whole

The land stays one piece. No subdivisions, no rows of rentals — just enough places to stay that it never feels crowded.

Built a little at a time

We add what the place needs, season by season, and we're upfront about what's ready and what isn't. If it's on the website as open, it's open.

Made for every age

Easy paths near parking and a single-level porch cottage in the works, alongside trails and campsites for the more adventurous. Grandparents and grandkids should both want to come.

Giving back to the land

Fraser firs go in the ground this November, with berries and bees to follow. Renewables are part of the plan, too. The property should leave the mountain better than it found it.

Benvenuti

Who comes up the mountain.

Campers, families, and small groups looking for somewhere quieter than town. Hosted gatherings open in 2027.

  • Weekend campers
  • Family reunions
  • Small weddings
  • Church retreats
  • Team retreats
  • Skiers staying close to the slopes
  • Anyone who needs a porch and a view
