The purpose

What this place is for.

Everything on the property is built around three things people come to the mountains for.

Slowing down

Porch views, shaded picnic tables, and a quiet garden for reading, prayer, and reflection.

Gathering

Fire pits, open ground, and a covered pavilion on the way — room for reunions, retreats, and dinners that run late.

Being outside

Trails through the woods, tent sites on the ridge, winter overnights, and a pond in the works.

Our approach

How the property is cared for.

A few commitments that shape every booking, event, and new addition.

Kept whole

The land stays one piece. No subdivisions, no rows of rentals — just enough places to stay that it never feels crowded.

Built a little at a time

New spaces arrive season by season, and the site says plainly what's open and what isn't. If it's marked open, it's open.

Made for every age

Easy paths near parking and a single-level porch cottage in the works, alongside trails and campsites for the adventurous.

Giving back to the land

Fraser firs go in this November, with berries and bees to follow, and renewables are part of the plan.
